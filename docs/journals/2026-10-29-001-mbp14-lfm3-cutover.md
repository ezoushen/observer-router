# The MBP-14 lane now serves the fine-tuned LFM3

2026-10-29. The `mbp14` tier no longer serves Gemma 4 E4B. It serves an LFM3-1.7B fine-tuned on
claude-mem's own observations and summaries, quantised to 6 bits, behind a reconciliation shim that
repairs summary replies before claude-mem validates them.

## Why the cutover happened

The bar agreed in advance was missed-worthy ≤ 4/36, invented paths ≤ 2/36, complete ≥ 32/32,
summaries ≥ 11/12 and wall p50/p90/TTFT ≤ 13.08 / 19.94 / 5.66 s, measured on the target machine.
Run solo on the M1, with production request settings and the shim in place:

| Measure | Bar | Gemma on MBP-14 | LFM3 q6 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Observers accepted | — | 36/36 | 36/36 |
| Missed worthy | ≤ 4/36 | 4/36 | 1/36 |
| Complete observations | ≥ 32/32 | 32/32 | 34/35 |
| Invented paths (full prompt) | ≤ 2/36 | 0 | 1/36 |
| Summaries passing the check | ≥ 11/12 | 11/12 | 12/12 |
| Wall p50 / p90 | ≤ 13.08 / 19.94 s | 13.08 / 19.94 | 7.26 / 9.44 |
| Time to first content | ≤ 5.66 s | 5.66 | 2.86 |

## Two measurements that were wrong

- **Two models on a 16 GB machine.** The first run kept Gemma resident on 8030 while scoring LFM3 on
  8032. The lane answered with Metal OOM — five observer requests returned 500 and the tail ran to
  33.89 s. That run was discarded rather than explained: the lane holds one model.
- **The shim buffered observer replies.** Reconciling cited paths before returning forced
  time-to-first-token to equal the whole reply (7.91 s). Splitting the paths — observers streamed
  through untouched, summaries buffered and reconciled — brought it to 2.86 s. The measured rate of
  genuinely invented paths is 1 in 36, so pass-through costs little.

## What changed

On MBP-14: `lfm3.py` from mlx-lm PR #2016 installed into the locals venv; the q6 model at
`~/models/lfm3-observer-q6`; `com.ezou.locals.lfm3.observer` bootstrapped on 8030 in place of
`com.ezou.locals.qwen4b.observer`, whose plist stays on the machine; `com.ezou.lfm-summarizer.shim`
on 8031 forwarding to it.

In the router: one provider block — `url` points at the shim, `model` names the lane's id, and
`min_budget_s` falls from 25 to 20 on the measured maximum of 18.7 s. `budget_s`, `serial`,
`chat_template_kwargs` and `buffer_stream` are unchanged.

## Verification

`/health` reports the chain with `mbp14 → locals/lfm3-observer-q6`. Through a throwaway router whose
chain named only `mbp14`, a real `grep` observer request returned one valid `<observation>` in 8.29 s.

## Rollback

`launchctl bootout gui/501/com.ezou.locals.lfm3.observer`, then `bootstrap` the old
`com.ezou.locals.qwen4b.observer.plist`, and restore `~/.config/observer-router/config.toml` from
`config.toml.before-lfm3-2026-10-29`. The shim can stay loaded; nothing points at it.
