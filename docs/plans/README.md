# Plans — observer router

Durable plans for work that spans more than one session: upstream contributions, migrations, and
decisions that need an owner's call before anything is filed or built.

A plan states an objective, its success criteria, what is explicitly **out of scope**, the
options considered with a recommendation, the evidence behind any claim, and the decision points
that block progress. The questions themselves are answered in `docs/researches/`; a plan is what
we intend to do about them.

| Date | Plan | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-09-20 | [One declared provider-resolution seam for claude-mem](2026-09-20-claude-mem-provider-resolution-seam.md) | Proposed — awaiting an owner decision on filing route and citation permission |

Once a plan is executed or abandoned, record the outcome in `docs/journals/` and leave the plan
in place with its status updated — plans are history, not drafts.
